  - num: "01"
    title: "What I work on"
    lead: "Physical AI — the body, not the mind. Integrator, not inventor."
    body: |
      I work on **physical AI** — making robots physically capable in the real world. Everyone is racing to give robots a better *mind*. I work on the harder, less crowded half: a *body* that can actually touch and handle the world. Models that look brilliant in a notebook still fall apart the moment they meet real hardware and real mess. Closing that gap is the part of robotics I care about most.

      Today I'm the **first robotics & ML engineer at [Sensetics](/experience/)**, a UC Berkeley spin-out building tactile sensing for humanoid robots. I built out the robotics function there — taking frontier, still-unproven sensing research and turning it into a working **sense of touch** on a real robot: integration, signal processing, and the unglamorous engineering that makes lab tech survive the physical world.

      There are two archetypes in this space. **Inventors** — the PhDs and physicists who invent the sensor and publish the paper. And **integrators** — the engineers who take that fragile, exotic lab tech and make a real robot actually *use* it. I'm firmly the second, and that's the seat I want.

  - num: "02"
    title: "The through-line"
    lead: "Perception and action where robots meet the messy physical world."
    body: |
      The thread across everything I've built is the same: **making robots perceive and act in the messy, unstructured real world.** Touch is just the newest instance of it, not a departure.

      I graduated from [IIT Patna](https://www.iitp.ac.in/) in 2022 with a B.Tech in [Mechanical Engineering](https://www.iitp.ac.in/index.php/departments/engineering-technology/mechanical-engineering/), where my capstone — a motion-prediction model for a towed floating object under [Prof. Subrata Kumar](https://www.iitp.ac.in/index.php/people-6/faculty/2-uncategorised/241-view-profile-38) — was recognized as an Undergraduate Project Finalist. I then earned my [M.S. in Robotics & Autonomous Systems](https://ras.engineering.asu.edu/) from [ASU](https://robotics.asu.edu/) in 2024 (distinction, A's across all ten courses), focused on perception, machine learning, and autonomous driving in the CARLA simulator, and co-authored a paper at **IEEE ACC 2024** in [Prof. Zhe Xu's](https://www.linkedin.com/in/xu-zhe/) lab.

      From there the work kept moving toward real robots in the real world: robotic software for precision agriculture at **Padma Agrobotics** (ROS2 systems, sensor and GPS-encoder fusion, on-field perception models), an LLM-driven humanoid behavior stack at **DreamFace Technologies**, and now tactile sensing for humanoids at **Sensetics**. Autonomous driving &rarr; aerial &rarr; agriculture &rarr; touch: different domains, one problem — perception and action at the point where a robot meets the physical world.

      Under my leadership, IIT Patna's team became [National Finalists](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1kweAUygwfA52OVF7uBK29grWofsJmhxy/view?usp=sharing) in the 2019–20 [e-Yantra Robotics Competition](https://portal.e-yantra.org/#about) (eYRC) at [IIT Bombay](https://www.iitb.ac.in/) — 4th of 1,051 teams — building an autonomous transport robot for disaster response.

      I later devised a simulator ecosystem for autonomous evaluation of the competition, earning recognition in the [e-YSIP program](https://www.e-yantra.org/eysip) under [Prof. Kavi Arya](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kavi-arya/?originalSubdomain=in) at IIT Bombay.

      My most recent public build is a [cup-stacking robot](/projects/cupstacking) from the NVIDIA × Seeed Embodied AI Hackathon — a public foundation model (GR00T 1.5 + ACT) taken all the way to a real arm on a Jetson Thor edge computer.
